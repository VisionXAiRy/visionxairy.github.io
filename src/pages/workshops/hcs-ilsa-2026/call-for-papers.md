# Call For Papers

## Objectives
The objective of this workshop is to explore how AI-enabled automation can bridge the gap with operational human work. We invite researchers and practitioners from academia and industry to discuss methods, systems, architectures, evaluations, and real-world experiences related to human-centered operational decision support in AI-enabled, pervasive, or cyber-physical environments.

## Target Audience
Target audiences include domain experts, academic researchers, industrial engineers, port and logistics operators, agronomists, and software developers working at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and cyber-physical systems.

## Topics of Interest
Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following areas:

* **Human-Centered AI:** Human-in-the-loop architectures, explainable, interactive, and contestable AI for expert users.
* **Contestability & Agency:** Systems that allow experts to question, override, or contextualize AI recommendations.
* **Pervasive Computing:** Mobile and ubiquitous interfaces for shop-floor, field, port, and control-room contexts.
* **Adaptive Digital Twins:** Action-oriented dashboards that connect data, prediction, explanation, and intervention.
* **XR & Multimodal Interaction:** Augmented and Mixed Reality for operator support and situated interfaces.
* **Cognitive Load & Sensemaking:** Tools for supporting operators who supervise collaborative robots, AGVs, and smart transit nodes.
* **Application Domains:** Industry 5.0, maritime ports, multimodal logistics, and precision agriculture.
