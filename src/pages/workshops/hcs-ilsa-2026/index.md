# HCS-ILSA 2026
**1st International Workshop on Human-Centered AI and Cyber-Physical Systems for Industry, Logistics, and Smart Agriculture**

Held in conjunction with the [17th International Conference on Emerging Ubiquitous Systems and Pervasive Networks (EUSPN 2026)](https://cs-conferences.acadiau.ca/euspn-26/)

**Location:** Almaty, Kazakhstan
**Dates:**  October 28-30, 2026

---

## About the Workshop
EUSPN 2026 provides an international forum for researchers and industry practitioners to share new ideas, original research results, and practical development experiences in ubiquitous systems and pervasive networks. **HCS-ILSA 2026** is proposed as a focused workshop within this scope, addressing human-centered operational decision support in AI-enabled cyber-physical environments.

AI-enabled cyber-physical systems, supported by IoT sensor networks, edge computing, pervasive computing infrastructures, digital twins, and predictive analytics, are transforming operational domains such as smart manufacturing, maritime ports, multimodal logistics, and precision agriculture. 

In real-world settings, accurate predictions may fail to support action if they are not explainable, contestable, timely, context-aware, or aligned with expert workflows. HCS-ILSA 2026 focuses on exploring how AI, ubiquitous and pervasive systems, XR, multimodal interfaces, explainable AI, adaptive digital twins, and context-aware systems can be designed, evaluated, and deployed to support expert agency, situational awareness, and responsible decision-making.
