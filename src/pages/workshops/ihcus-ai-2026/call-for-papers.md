# Call For Papers

## Objectives
The objective of this workshop is to explore how AI-enabled automation can bridge the gap with operational human work within agricultural and industrial contexts. We invite researchers and practitioners from academia and industry to discuss methods, systems, architectures, evaluations, and real-world experiences related to human-centered operational decision support in AI-enabled, pervasive, and cyber-physical environments.

## Target Audience
Target audiences include domain experts, academic researchers, industrial engineers, agronomists, agritech specialists, and software developers working at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), artificial intelligence, and cyber-physical systems.

## Topics of Interest
Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following areas:

* **Human-Centered AI:** Human-in-the-loop (HITL) architectures, along with explainable, interactive, and contestable AI for expert users.
* **Contestability & Agency:** Systems that allow industrial operators and agricultural experts to question, override, or contextualize AI recommendations.
* **Pervasive Computing:** Mobile, ubiquitous, and context-aware interfaces for shop-floor, field, and control-room environments.
* **Adaptive Digital Twins:** Action-oriented dashboards that connect sensor data, predictive analytics, explanation, and intervention in farming and manufacturing.
* **XR & Multimodal Interaction:** Augmented and Mixed Reality (AR/MR) for operator support, remote maintenance, agronomist assistance, and situated interfaces.
* **Cognitive Ergonomics & Sensemaking:** Tools for managing cognitive load and supporting workers who supervise collaborative robots (cobots), autonomous agricultural machinery, and automated guided vehicles (AGVs).
* **Application Domains:** Industry 5.0, smart manufacturing, Agriculture 4.0, precision farming, and connected agrifood supply chains.
