## About the Workshop
EUSPN 2026 provides an international forum for researchers and industry practitioners to share new ideas, original research results, and practical development experiences in ubiquitous systems and pervasive networks. **IHCUS-AI 2026** is proposed as a focused workshop within this scope, addressing human-centered operational decision support in AI-enabled cyber-physical environments.

AI-enabled cyber-physical systems, supported by IoT sensor networks, edge computing, pervasive infrastructures, and predictive analytics, are fundamentally transforming operational domains. In the context of **Industry 5.0** and **Agriculture 4.0**, this transformation goes beyond mere automation; it demands resilient, sustainable, and fundamentally human-centric operations. Environments such as smart manufacturing floors, connected agrifood supply chains, and precision farming landscapes increasingly rely on advanced technologies like vision-based AI, collaborative robotics (cobots), and multimodal sensor fusion. 

However, in complex real-world settings, highly accurate predictive models may fail to support practical action if they are not explainable, contestable, context-aware, or seamlessly aligned with expert workflows—from factory floor operators to field agronomists. **IHCUS-AI 2026** focuses on exploring how Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) AI, ubiquitous systems, extended reality (XR), multimodal interfaces, and adaptive digital twins can be designed, evaluated, and deployed. The ultimate goal is to support expert agency, manage cognitive load, enhance situational awareness, and ensure responsible decision-making across agricultural and industrial workspaces.
