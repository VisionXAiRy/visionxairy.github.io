# Team

He is the coordinator of the VisionXAIR Lab, a cutting-edge research hub where computer vision, artificial intelligence, and extended reality (XR) converge to shape human-centered technology. Having served as the Head of the SustainRD Research Center (2019-2023), he holds a PhD in Computer Science from the NOVA School of Science and Technology. His research expertise lies in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Digital Health, Digital Twins, Gamification, and the personalization of ubiquitous computing applications.
